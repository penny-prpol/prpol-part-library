## square truss panel

covers a truss face composed of four hubs in a square with leg struts joining 
them. The most straightforward panel type. 

The "gaps" boolean controls whether there the panel tiles a face of a truss 
structure with or without gaps in between adjacent panels. If there are gaps, that leaves room for struts to continue outwards from that face of the truss structure.
